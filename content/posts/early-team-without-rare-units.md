# Castle Busters Early Team Guide: Free Hero Access and Unit Limits

**Short answer:** Patch 1.16 labels Sir Rally free at Arena 7, Leif free at Arena 25, and Wolfie Battle Pass Exclusive. These dated labels do not prove current ownership or a non-rare team. The loadout limit is up to eight distinct units, not a required early lineup.

## Free and early are different access labels

The table repeats Castle Busters patch 1.16. It does not extend these labels to later versions, platforms, seasons, or accounts. Confirm availability in your live client.

| Hero | Patch 1.16 access label | What it does not guarantee |
|---|---|---|
| Sir Rally | Arena 7 · Free | Current ownership or access before Arena 7 |
| Leif | Arena 25 · Free | An Arena 7 free starter |
| Wolfie | Battle Pass Exclusive | Current season, price, or a later acquisition route |

## Eight units is a limit, not an early roster prescription

The official overview says a loadout can contain up to eight distinct units. This is only a capacity ceiling. It does not prove eight early, free, or non-rare units are available, or require you to own, field, or fund eight immediately.

These sources do not support a complete recipe. Rarity, strength, costs, substitutes, and results remain unverified here.

## Check what your account actually has

1. Write down only the units the current account shows as owned or immediately available.
2. Keep a patch label separate from account ownership. A Free label does not prove the hero is already collected.
3. Record the arena and client version visible on the account before using a dated label.
4. Leave rarity, price, unlock steps, later routes, and an exact composition unknown without accepted evidence.
