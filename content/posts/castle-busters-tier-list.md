The official site advertises 30+ units but does not publish a complete current roster or comparable stat table. This page ranks evidence confidence and role fit rather than inventing a universal S-to-D order.

## Verified ability tier

Bishop, Wrecker, Fairy, Pulsar, Saxton, Sir Rally, Leif, Wolfie and Graves have publicly described attacks or abilities. Vampire is named in a patch note but does not have enough public behavior detail for a performance rank.

## Choose by the failure

Use Wrecker or Fairy as area-damage candidates, Pulsar or Saxton when a line or return path matters, Sir Rally for immediately adjacent support, Leif for a same-row revival condition, Wolfie for a neighboring-death trigger and Graves for persistent board pressure. These are hypotheses to test, not universal rankings.

## Performance-tier gate

Record the patch, target, formation, upgrade state and at least two comparable results. Compare units tested for the same job. Patch 1.18 changes Wolfie and foundation behavior, so older conclusions involving those systems need a new baseline.
