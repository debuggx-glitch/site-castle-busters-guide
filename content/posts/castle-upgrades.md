Upgrade the castle only when the castle or its structures are the observed bottleneck. Make the smallest change that could affect the failure, replay a comparable fight and stop if the same failure returns.

## Diagnose before spending

If units disappear first, return to loadout or formation. If the castle breaks while useful units remain, inspect exposure, foundations, movement, defensive helpers and material tier.

## Materials are not a universal shopping order

The official overview confirms material progression from wood and stone through higher tiers including diamond and magma. It does not publish a universal first-buy order or every current value. Read the client, record the before state and buy one step.

## Retest foundations after 1.18

Patch 1.18 changes foundations into active structural reinforcement. A new position may alter the result without a wider material spend. Change one foundation variable and stop that branch if the same structure fails at the same stage.
