Patch 1.18 adds Graves, reworks foundations and changes several hero and battle behaviors. Any formation or upgrade result recorded before 1.18 should be retested when it depends on those systems.

## Graves

Graves throws tombstones that bounce until they settle. At the end of the turn, each grave fires a ghost and exposed units are targeted first. Graves remain until a projectile or rolling castle destroys them. The public note does not publish damage, range, health, rarity or an optimal position.

## Foundation rework

Foundations now actively reinforce structures. The official goal is to reduce unpredictable chain collapses and make placement matter more. Save the old layout, change one foundation position, and compare the same structure under the current version.

## Hero and battle fixes

- Wolfie explosion scaling was fixed and his transformation damage boost increased.
- Leif and Sir Rally explosion-power problems were fixed.
- Anti-cheat and PvP synchronization were improved.
- Laser colors, Pulsar’s stuck laser, training displays and several shop or battle interface problems were fixed.

The Ice Blaster scaling issue is described as coming soon, not as completed in this patch.
